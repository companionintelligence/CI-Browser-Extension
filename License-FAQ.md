# License FAQ

### Can you summarize what the Companion Browser Extension's license allows?

The license grants free rights to use, modify, and redistribute the Companion Browser Extension source for personal or nonprofit purposes. The intent is to stay permissive for those uses while limiting commercial abuse of Companion Intelligence products.

The Companion Browser Extension uses the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0). The full text is in [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md).

### What freedoms do you have as a Companion Browser Extension user?

If you use the extension for personal or nonprofit purposes, you can view, modify, and redistribute the source under the same license terms, subject to the PolyForm Noncommercial restrictions.

### Can I fork the Companion Browser Extension?

Yes. For noncommercial use, you can fork, modify, and redistribute this repository under the same license.

### Can I contribute to the Companion Browser Extension?

Yes. Open a pull request on this repository.

### Can I sell the Companion Browser Extension?

No. Selling the extension, including listing a paid fork in a store or selling paid support for those products, is not permitted. For commercial partnership questions, email partner@companionintelligence.com.

### Does this license apply to Companion Portal?

No. Companion Portal stays a separate commercial product. Companion Hub, Companion Memory, Companion Capture, Companion Marketplace, and Companion OS use the same PolyForm Noncommercial terms in their own repositories.
